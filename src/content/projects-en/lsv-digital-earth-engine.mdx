## Context

LocaSpace Viewer (LSV) is a desktop digital earth application for transportation planning, engineering construction and related industries. Users need to load terrain, imagery, vector data and models onto a 3D globe, then view, measure, analyze and export results.

## My work

I worked on feature R&D for the digital earth engine, mainly involving:

- data loading and display in 3D scenes;
- overlaying terrain, imagery, vector data and models;
- basic measurement and analysis tools;
- feature customization and issue diagnosis for industry projects.

## Technical environment

Desktop C++ engines and browser engines have different priorities:

- desktop engines care more about large datasets and long-running stability;
- the render pipeline is closer to the metal and debugging is more expensive;
- industry users have stricter requirements for coordinates, precision and formats.

This experience exposed me to the combination of **real GIS data and 3D rendering**, rather than pure graphics effects.

## Outcomes

- Contributed to multiple feature modules of the digital earth engine.
- Supported real projects in transportation planning and engineering construction.
- Built a foundation in 3D GIS data loading, rendering and interaction.

## Reflection

LSV was my starting point in 3D GIS. Looking back, the most important takeaway was not a specific feature, but understanding the relationship between engine capabilities, industry data and project delivery. Many later decisions in WebGL engines and HD map production came from this experience.
