I'm XIAO AN, a 3D geospatial engineer. I focus on rendering engines, GIS geometry, 3D modeling and HD map data production.

I like thinking about rendering, geometry and data production as one system: how data is organized, how geometry is computed, and how it finally renders and responds.

This site is where I document engineering practice, keep technical notes and publish a few spatial utilities.
